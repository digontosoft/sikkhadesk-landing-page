# SikkhaDesk search visibility audit

Reviewed on 11 October 2026, using the Bangladesh client date. Scope: the local landing-page repository, public production HTML and HTTP responses, a sample of competing pages, and current primary documentation for Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic. This is an audit and implementation brief; application code and production settings were not changed.

**Assessment**

SikkhaDesk has useful foundations: publicly accessible pages, Bangla product content, actual feature descriptions, published student-based prices, institution names and logos, testimonials, a dashboard screenshot, and contact and policy pages. The main content and FAQ answers are already in the initial production HTML. Client components have not turned this into an empty page that depends entirely on JavaScript for its text.

The biggest opportunity is to make the product's category, Bangladesh relevance, and evidence much clearer, then provide substantial answers to buyers' questions. Metadata fixes alone will not make a small marketing site consistently outrank established vendors or make AI assistants recommend it.

Prioritize Bangladesh searches and specific product workflows before treating the worldwide phrase “school management software” as the primary success measure. Actual Google positions, keyword demand, backlinks, historical traffic, and AI mention share were not measured. Search results sampled through the research tool are discovery evidence, not a Bangladesh-localized Google ranking report. No ranking or AI placement is guaranteed.

**1. State what the product is immediately**

Evidence: [metadata helper](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/lib/metadata.ts:16), [site configuration](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/constants/site.ts:3), and [hero content](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/sections/hero/hero-content.tsx:45). The live homepage title and H1 confirm the same wording. The description mentions Bangladesh, but the title is a general slogan and the main heading focuses on easier administration. The visible homepage text does not contain the English phrase “school management software.”

Recommended copy direction:

- English page title: `School Management Software in Bangladesh | SikkhaDesk`.
- English H1: `School management software built for Bangladesh`.
- Bangla H1: `বাংলাদেশের স্কুল ম্যানেজমেন্ট সফটওয়্যার — SikkhaDesk`.
- Opening explanation: name the supported institution types and explain student records, attendance, fees, exams, and communication in concrete language.

These are positioning examples, not an exact-match keyword requirement. Keep the copy natural and consistent with actual capabilities. Google can understand related wording. Each language version should have its own appropriate metadata.

**2. Replace short descriptions with buyer-level explanations**

Evidence: [feature data](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/constants/features.ts:18), [Features page](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/app/features/page.tsx:13), and [Pricing page](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/app/pricing/page.tsx:13). Features is the same short card grid shown on the homepage; Pricing repeats the same pricing section and FAQs. This is shared content, not automatically a duplicate-content penalty. The issue is how little additional information those pages provide.

For each important workflow, show the problem, how SikkhaDesk handles it, screenshots, supported reports, limitations, and a relevant demo. Start with fee collection and dues, attendance, and exams/results. Explain imports from Excel, onboarding, staff training, data export, support, and migration where supported. Distinguish payments for a SikkhaDesk subscription from any payment integration used by guardians to pay school fees.

Institution-specific pages should explain real differences for schools, madrasas, and coaching centers. Footer links for these institution types currently all go to Contact: [navigation data](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/constants/navigation.ts:32). Do not create pages for every city or keyword variant with substantially the same text.

**3. Correct the business identity and reconcile claims**

Evidence: [footer address](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/layout/footer.tsx:59) gives Mymensingh, while the [Contact address](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/sections/contact/contact.tsx:43) gives Uttara, Dhaka. Both appear in production. Confirm the real address; if both are genuine offices, label their roles clearly.

Support is described as Sunday–Thursday, 9am–7pm in the footer, 9am–8pm with Friday closed on Contact, 9am–9pm in the FAQ section, and 24/7 in other claims. Explain whether these are phone hours, ticket availability, or plan-dependent response commitments.

The [testimonials section](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/sections/testimonials/testimonials.tsx:17) advertises a 4.8/5 rating and a 95% reuse rate without a source, date, sample size, or definition. Seven stored testimonials all have five-star ratings; the 4.8 figure may come from another dataset, but that dataset is not identified. The footer says 3,000+ users and the testimonials section says 3,300+; compatible lower bounds, but worth standardizing.

Confirm the advertised Android/iOS apps, offline synchronization, encryption, trial terms, and dedicated support before expanding these claims. Add official app links where available. The repository also contains payment account details that look like example values: [checkout configuration](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/constants/checkout.ts:35). Verify these before customers use the checkout. This observation does not establish that the accounts are invalid.

Evidence: [hero buttons](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/sections/hero/hero-buttons.tsx:10) and [feature call to action](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/sections/features/features.tsx:82), also confirmed in production HTML:

- “সব ফিচার দেখুন” leads to Pricing.

The footer also labels Contact links as help center and usage instructions. Supply actual guides or use accurate labels. These are navigation and conversion gaps, rather than confirmed ranking penalties.

**6. Make the total price quotable and unambiguous**

Evidence: [price-unit condition](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/sections/pricing/pricing-card.tsx:56) displays a yearly unit but no monthly unit. [Checkout calculation](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/lib/checkout.ts:53) adds a configured 15% VAT to subscription plus setup fees; the homepage and Pricing content do not explain that treatment.

For the Basic 0–150-student monthly selection, the code calculates BDT 500 subscription + BDT 2,000 setup + BDT 375 VAT = BDT 2,875 initially. This describes the application's calculation, not the legally applicable tax rate.

Show “BDT 500/month for up to 150 students,” the one-time setup fee, whether displayed prices include tax, and the first payable amount. Explain what differs between plans: Basic says all features, while other copy limits multi-branch access to Premium/Custom. Publish a readable comparison table and specific trial start instructions.

**7–9. Establish measurement and consistent crawl signals**

Evidence: the [metadata helper](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/lib/metadata.ts:20) contains no canonical configuration. Site configuration uses the non-www host even though production redirects to www. No sitemap/robots source files were found, and their standard production endpoints return 404.

Recommended actions:

1. Keep the existing working www host unless there is a deliberate domain change. Use it consistently in metadata, canonical URLs, structured-data identifiers, sitemaps, and public profiles.
2. Add self-referencing canonicals for indexable pages with the production trailing-slash convention. Open Graph URLs do not replace canonical tags. The existing 308 redirect is already a useful canonicalization signal. [Google canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls).
3. Publish a sitemap containing canonical, successful, indexable URLs. Use meaningful modification dates. Add it to robots.txt and submit it in Search Console. A sitemap helps discovery; it does not guarantee indexing. [Google sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview).
4. Apply `noindex` to Checkout, including its query-parameter selections, and omit it from the sitemap. Leave it crawlable so crawlers can read noindex. Protect private product areas separately; robots.txt is not authentication.
5. Verify a Search Console domain property, inspect key URLs, and check Google's selected canonicals and indexing status. Verification can be done through DNS, so the lack of a verification tag does not prove that Search Console is absent.
6. Track qualified demo requests, contact submissions, WhatsApp clicks, and checkout starts/completions. No analytics integration was found in the inspected source or HTML; hosting-side instrumentation and existing accounts were not inspected.

**10–11. Add coherent language versions and accurate structured data**

Evidence: [root layout](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/app/layout.tsx:22) declares English for Bangla marketing pages, while [metadata](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/lib/metadata.ts:29) uses the US English Open Graph locale. There are no language alternate URLs. English legal pages have an appropriate English body, so language changes need to consider individual routes.

Keep the current Bangla content at the existing URLs and create a complete `/en/` version of important marketing pages. Use the correct HTML language, self-canonicals, reciprocal `bn-BD`/`en-BD` hreflang, and an intentional default-language choice. Language tags alone are not a ranking shortcut; Google also identifies language from visible content. [Google multilingual guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites).

Add Organization data with the verified company identity, contacts, logo, and official profiles. Describe SikkhaDesk with SoftwareApplication data and actual supported features/offers. Connect the operator and product if their relationship is verified. Add breadcrumb data to deeper pages. Structured data must agree with the page.

Google's software rich result requires a price and a qualifying rating or review, among other requirements. Do not turn the site's unsubstantiated 4.8 statistic into an aggregate rating simply to pass validation. Basic entity description and rich-result eligibility are different goals. [Google software application documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/software-app).

**12–15. Improve proof, headings, content resilience, and supporting details**

The customer names and institutional logos are valuable starting points. Obtain permission and create two or three case studies with institution type, location, workflows used, implementation dates, screenshots, attributable quotes, and documented outcomes. Link legitimate customer references where possible. Seek genuine independent reviews and relevant editorial mentions; no backlink inventory was available, so this audit does not establish that SikkhaDesk lacks backlinks.

Features, Pricing, and Contact should have descriptive page-level H1s instead of only reused homepage section headings. This improves structure and clarity; absence of an H1 alone does not explain a ranking position.

The [hero animation](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/sections/hero/hero-content.tsx:18), feature cards, and pricing cards start at opacity zero. This is a resilience and potential rendering-performance concern, not evidence that their HTML is missing. Make essential content readable when animation fails or reduced motion is requested. The [testimonial carousel](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/sections/testimonials/testimonials.tsx:60) initially renders only four of seven stored testimonials; complete case-study pages would provide stable accessible references. Closed FAQ answers already remain in HTML, which is positive.

Add an actual share image; the declared large Twitter card currently has none. This is primarily a sharing improvement. The [newsletter form](C:/digonto/sikkhadesk-landing-page-main/components/layout/newsletter-form.tsx:9) only changes local state and shows success without storing the email. Connect it to a subscription service or remove the success claim.

**Visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude**

These systems do not provide one universal keyword ranking. Search-enabled recommendations, citations, referral traffic, and accurate brand descriptions are separate measurable outcomes from a model mentioning a company without web search.

| Surface | Action | Scope and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT search | Ensure OAI-SearchBot can fetch public pages and that the CDN permits its published IP ranges | Official OpenAI Docs distinguishes search access from GPTBot's potential training access; ChatGPT-User is a separate user-requested fetcher. Actual verified bot traffic was not tested. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots). |
| Claude search | Avoid blocking Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User on public marketing content | ClaudeBot serves a separate model-development purpose. Crawler permission improves access, not guaranteed recommendation. [Anthropic crawler documentation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler). |
| Google Search AI features | Verify indexing/snippet eligibility and the Search Console Search generative AI inclusion setting | Google documents a default inclusion setting, with parent-property inheritance; this website's actual account setting was not inspected. [Search generative AI control](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024). |
| Gemini Apps | Review the Google-Extended policy in addition to Googlebot access | Google-Extended covers specified training and grounding uses in Gemini Apps and Vertex AI; it is not a Google Search ranking signal. [Google crawler controls](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#google-extended). |

Supply trustworthy answers about who the product serves, real costs, supported workflows, implementation, data export, and documented customer outcomes. This recommendation comes from the observed buyer-information gaps, rather than a claim that AI systems require a special writing format. Maintain consistent product facts on official profiles and legitimate third-party references.

Google states that it ignores llms.txt for Search and that special AI markup is unnecessary. Treat llms.txt as an optional integration aid, not an SEO fix. Google's current documentation also says FAQ rich results stopped appearing from 7 May 2026; useful FAQs remain worthwhile, but FAQ schema is not a route to a Google FAQ rich result. [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).

**Competitor observations**

The sampled pages show useful content patterns, not verified top positions or verified product capabilities:

- Rofasware directly names the Bangladesh school-software category and explains attendance, fees/accounts, exams, and buyer FAQs. [Rofasware landing page](https://school.rofasware.com/).
- IskulAI provides prominent local suitability, starting price/trial claims, detailed workflow explanations, and navigation to features, comparisons, guides, and a blog. [IskulAI landing page](https://iskulai.com/).

**Implementation order and acceptance checks**

First cycle: establish the verified facts and measurement baseline; improve category copy; correct links and price explanations; compress images; align host/canonicals; publish sitemap and robots policy; mark Checkout noindex. Many of these are small changes and can ship together.

Next cycle: expand Features and Pricing, add accurate entity data and page headings, then publish the English commercial pages and the first three detailed module pages. Follow with customer case studies, operator/security information, and institution pages supported by real product differences.

Over the following 30–90 days: earn genuine customer references and relevant coverage, answer actual buyer questions, and adjust the content plan using observed search queries and lead quality. This is a work schedule, not a promise of top ranking within 90 days.

Acceptance checks after implementation:

- Canonical marketing pages return 200 and advertise the same www/trailing-slash URLs as the sitemap; alternate hosts redirect consistently.
- Robots and sitemap return 200 with valid content; Checkout is crawlable but noindex and absent from the sitemap.
- Search Console confirms indexing status and selected canonical URLs; generative AI inclusion is checked rather than assumed.
- Each language version has correct content, metadata, H1, and reciprocal language alternates.
- Structured data matches visible facts and passes the relevant validators without invented reviews.
- Demo, feature, support, and pricing links reach the promised destination; lead events record correctly.
- Mobile performance is measured after image and animation changes. Review field Core Web Vitals where sufficient data exists.
- Track Bangladesh non-brand impressions/clicks, qualified organic demos, and AI referral conversions. Use Google's documented generative AI impressions report where available; low-impression sites may not yet display it. [Search Console generative AI performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139).
- For ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude, repeat a fixed set of representative search-enabled prompts in fresh sessions. Record date, wording, search mode, cited sources, brand mention, and factual accuracy. Report the observed sample rather than treating one answer as a stable rank.

**Limits of this audit**

Source review and production HTTP/HTML checks were completed. Production HTML samples were saved in the system temporary directory during inspection. No application build, Lighthouse run, mobile visual audit, authenticated product test, live form submission, payment, or deployment was performed. Search Console, analytics, verified crawler logs, complete backlink data, and product-claim evidence were unavailable. Consequently, speed scores, indexing history, current Google positions, and verified AI recommendation frequency remain open measurements.
