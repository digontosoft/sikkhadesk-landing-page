# SikkhaDesk: competitor content and SEO gaps

Reviewed: 11 October 2026, Bangladesh time.

## Main finding

The next opportunity is **more specific product evidence, broader coverage of buyer questions, and verified company/customer information**. SikkhaDesk already has a local technical SEO implementation; repeating metadata work will not close these content gaps.

The comparison below uses the current local landing-page project and public vendor pages. It is a content audit, not a Bangladesh-localized Google rank, search-volume, backlink or market-share measurement. Vendor capability and customer claims are self-published and were not independently authenticated. Suggested priorities are editorial judgment, not measured traffic forecasts.

I could not retrieve the live SikkhaDesk robots file, sitemap or support site with the research fetcher. This does not establish that those services are offline. Deployment and live indexing therefore remain unconfirmed in this review.

## Current SikkhaDesk baseline

The sitemap lists 10 indexable routes: two homepages, features, pricing, contact, two legal pages and three module pages. Checkout is intentionally excluded and noindexed. The detailed modules are fee management, attendance management and exam/results.

The feature grid contains 12 feature cards, while only three module detail pages exist. Accounting and balance-sheet cards may belong on a combined page; one new page per card is not automatically necessary.

The project already includes canonical URLs, homepage language alternates, Organization/WebSite/SoftwareApplication structured data, breadcrumbs, crawlable initial content, optimized images, transparent subscription/setup/VAT totals and conversion tracking. These should be retained and verified on the deployed site.

The Bangla homepage displays institution logos, but does not provide case studies or linked institution profiles. Unverified quotes and ratings have been removed. `/en/` is an English overview; there are no equivalent English feature/module/pricing routes yet. A support subdomain is linked, but its content could not be inspected here.

## Priority 1: improve the evidence on existing pages

### 1. Demonstrate the three existing modules

Each module needs an actual product screenshot and a complete example with fictitious demonstration data:

- Fees: configure a fee, record collection, issue a receipt, review outstanding dues and a collection report.
- Attendance: show how records are entered and how an attendance report is viewed. State which device/SMS automation options are actually available.
- Results: show marks entry and the resulting marksheet/tabulation output. Explain supported grading rules after checking the application.

Add short Bangla walkthrough videos with text instructions or transcripts. Label sample data clearly. A sample receipt or marksheet should reflect a real output format and contain no real student records. Add video structured data only when a real playable video and accurate metadata exist.

Some current module text asks buyers to confirm capabilities during the demo. That is appropriate where details are unknown, but verified implementation details would make these pages substantially more useful.

### 2. Publish company and customer evidence

Proposed routes: `/about/`, then `/customers/` and `/case-studies/[institution-slug]/` when evidence is ready.

The About page should identify the actual operator, its relationship to the SikkhaDesk brand, people responsible for product/support, confirmed Mymensingh address and support hours, and official public profiles. Verify legal names and credentials before publication.

For a case study, collect the institution's permission, name, type, location, date of adoption, actual modules used and a specific before/after workflow. Quantified outcomes require a measurement method, dates and institution confirmation. A factual account without invented percentages is sufficient. Link to an institution's official site where relevant and authorized.

Confirm the status and permission of existing institution logos. Turn verified logos into named, useful references. Do not infer approval for quotes or performance claims from possession of a logo.

### 3. Clarify operational and integration details

Expand existing pricing/FAQ content with confirmed answers about:

- What the setup charge includes; migration responsibilities, supported import formats, training and realistic launch steps.
- SMS purchase and usage costs, payment-gateway requirements/charges, supported attendance devices and any website/domain charges.
- Data export, cancellation, retention after expiry, backup frequency and recovery procedure.
- Which roles and tasks work in the Android app, browser and any offline mode.

Use a support matrix: **available now / optional integration / not available**. Do not label planned work as available. Collect engineering and commercial confirmation before asserting automated bKash/Nagad reconciliation, biometric support, guardian access, Hifz tracking or particular grading standards.

Current FAQ promises about offline sync and data protection still need verification. Avoid absolute claims that data cannot be lost. Checkout's existing payment-account details also require business verification before public use; changing SEO text cannot solve that issue.

## Priority 3: cover institution and language differences

Proposed routes: `/solutions/madrasa-management/` and `/solutions/coaching-management/`.

The madrasa page should explain the workflows SikkhaDesk actually supports for that audience, using real examples. The coaching page should demonstrate supported batch, student, fee and exam processes. If the product currently uses general class structures, describe that honestly. Hifz, Kitab, donations, boarding, special curricula or teacher revenue sharing require separate confirmation.

Do not duplicate a page by replacing “school” with “madrasa” or “coaching.” Do not generate district pages without distinct local evidence. Keep university/advanced LMS targeting outside the initial backlog unless those capabilities and customers exist.

Add substantive English equivalents for features, pricing and the three existing modules. Give each translation its own canonical URL and reciprocal language alternates. Link translations visibly. The intent is to answer English-speaking buyers' follow-up questions after `/en/`, not to promise that English itself improves rankings.

## Priority 4: publish useful guides

Start with four original guides, each linked to relevant modules and pricing:

1. **School management software cost in Bangladesh**: subscription, setup, VAT, SMS, hardware and renewal; explain cost categories and use verified SikkhaDesk examples. Named competitor prices need dated primary references.
2. **How to choose school management software in Bangladesh**: a practical demo checklist and a comparison worksheet; acknowledge who may be adequately served by spreadsheets.
3. **Moving school records from Excel to management software**: actual supported import fields, validation, responsibilities and reconciliation. Supply a template only after confirming the real import format.
4. **Managing school fees and overdue payments**: a worked example using the real fee module, with sample receipts and reports.

Suggested route family: `/guides/[slug]/`. If the existing support site already covers these tasks, inspect and connect its resources before creating duplicates. Give guides a real author/reviewer and substantive review date. Avoid generic articles explaining why technology is good, invented “best software” rankings and changing the year without reviewing content.

## Google and AI discovery

Readable facts, accessible pages and evidence are the common foundation. Do not make “number one” promises or describe AI inclusion as a fixed ranking position.

Google says its AI Overviews/AI Mode use the usual SEO requirements, with no extra AI text file or special schema required. Indexed, snippet-eligible pages, internal links, textual content and relevant images/videos matter. This guidance concerns Google Search AI features; it is not a guarantee about every AI assistant. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

Official OpenAI documentation identifies `OAI-SearchBot` as the crawler for ChatGPT search, distinct from training through `GPTBot`. Check that the production host/CDN permits legitimate search requests; an allow rule in source code does not establish live access. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots).

Anthropic distinguishes `Claude-SearchBot` for search and `Claude-User` for user-directed retrieval from its training crawler. Check production access for the discovery/retrieval paths. [Anthropic crawler documentation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler).

Google documents `Google-Extended` as controlling content use for Gemini training and grounding, separately from Google Search inclusion/ranking. It has no independent HTTP user-agent string. Do not mistake this setting for a Google ranking boost. [Google crawler documentation](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#google-extended).

The local robots implementation currently allows all user agents. There is no demonstrated need to add separate repetitive allow groups. Verify live delivery and hosting rules before treating crawler access as a new code gap. Prioritize useful public pages and legitimate references over an `llms.txt` experiment; no cited documentation establishes that such a file would secure rankings across these assistants.

Build consistent official company/app profiles and obtain relevant editorial or institution references from real relationships. Their effect on AI selection is an inference, not a documented inclusion guarantee. No outreach, account changes or directory submissions were performed.

## Sitemap and measurement

`app/sitemap.ts` already discovers module URLs from `productModules`. Add other route families as they are actually implemented and publishable. Do not add proposed URLs now: a sitemap entry without a working substantive page does not fill a content gap. Retain checkout's exclusion and add matched language alternates only for real translations.

After deployment, verify status codes, canonical redirects, robots delivery and live page rendering. Use Search Console to establish actual indexed pages, Bangladesh query impressions, clicks and position trends. Pair those with GA4 leads and demo clicks. Track the broad English phrases and natural Bangla equivalents separately; the unqualified global phrase is a different competitive target from the Bangladesh-specific query.

Record a small repeatable set of Bangla/English buyer questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude with search enabled where available, noting date, cited URLs and whether SikkhaDesk appears. Treat this as a sampled observation: responses vary, absence is not a definitive indexing diagnosis, and referral analytics does not measure every AI mention.

## Recommended order

1. Verify live deployment/indexing, product claims and payment details; collect screenshots and company/customer evidence.
2. Improve the three existing module pages and publish a factual About page.
3. Add student-management and SMS pages, then English feature/pricing/module equivalents.
4. Publish the first permission-backed case study; develop madrasa/coaching pages from verified workflows.
5. Add the cost/selection guides, tutorials and remaining module details, using query and lead data to revise priorities.

Only this research document was added during this review. Application code and sitemap routes were not changed.
